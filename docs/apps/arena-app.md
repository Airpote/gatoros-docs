# Arena App

The Arena App provides direct integration with the Arena.social platform, bringing social features, community interactions, and Gator token ecosystem management into GatorOS.

## Features

### Social Integration
- **Arena Feed**: Real-time community posts and updates
- **Social Profiles**: User profiles and reputation system
- **Community Posts**: Create and share content
- **Like/Comment System**: Interact with community content

### Gator Token Management
- **Token Balance**: View GATOR holdings
- **Staking Interface**: Stake tokens for rewards
- **Reward Claims**: Harvest staking rewards
- **Governance**: Participate in protocol decisions

### Community Features
- **Live Chat**: Real-time community messaging
- **Event Calendar**: Community events and AMAs
- **Leaderboard**: Top community contributors
- **Achievement System**: Earn badges and rewards

## How to Use

1. **Connect Arena Account**: Link your Arena.social account
2. **View Feed**: Browse community posts and updates
3. **Interact**: Like, comment, and share content
4. **Manage Tokens**: Stake GATOR and claim rewards
5. **Participate**: Join community discussions
